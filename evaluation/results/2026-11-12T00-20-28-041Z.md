## How the metrics are computed

- **Accuracy** = correct answers ÷ questions asked. An answer is correct when the intent the bot answered with is one of the question's expected intents.
- For each intent *c*: **Precision** = TP ÷ (TP + FP), **Recall** = TP ÷ (TP + FN), **F1** = 2 × P × R ÷ (P + R), where TP counts questions about *c* answered as *c*, FP questions about another intent answered as *c*, and FN questions about *c* answered as anything else (another intent or the fallback).
- The **macro** averages are the means over the tested intents, each counting equally.
- A fallback ("I didn't understand") counts as a wrong answer.
